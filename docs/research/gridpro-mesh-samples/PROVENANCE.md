# Provenance of the extracted mesh figures

One line per file. Only **open-licence** sources were extracted; every paywalled or
all-rights-reserved source in `../gridpro-mesh-data-in-publications.md` is recorded as a
**pointer only**. The licence each file was taken under is named.

- `entry06-vatsa1995-fig1-97block-multielement-airfoil.png` — V. N. Vatsa, M. D. Sanetrik,
  E. B. Parlette, *Block-structured grids for complex aerodynamic configurations: Current status*,
  NASA CP-3291 (Surface Modeling, Grid Generation, and Related Issues in CFD Solutions, 1995),
  **page 11 (printed p. 173), Figure 1**, "Partial view of grid for 97-block multielement airfoil
  configuration". Source PDF `https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19950022315/downloads/19950022315.pdf`.
  Licence: NTRS record `19950022315` reports `determinationType: GOV_PUBLIC_USE_PERMITTED`,
  `distribution: PUBLIC`, `disseminated: DOCUMENT_AND_METADATA`. Rendered from the PDF page at
  300 dpi and cropped to the figure (1520x1260 px).

- `entry07-eiseman1995-fig4-airfoil-in-ground-effect.png` — P. R. Eiseman (Program Development
  Corp.), *Multiblock grid generation with automatic zoning*, NASA CP-3291, 1995, **page 14
  (printed p. 156), Figure 4**, "An airfoil close to the ground." Source PDF
  `https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19950022314/downloads/19950022314.pdf`. Licence: NTRS record
  `19950022314` reports `determinationType: GOV_PUBLIC_USE_PERMITTED`, `distribution: PUBLIC`.
  **Vendor-authored [V-GP]**. Rendered at 300 dpi and cropped to the two-panel figure
  (1860x1740 px).
