(4) WHAT IS *NOT* REPRODUCIBLE, AND IS SAID SO IN THE NOTE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * The pre-fix one-sided-tangent figure in the note's SS2(a) -- the O-grid's
    unsmoothed wall first-cell error at 0.024536 -- was measured against a
    version of the prototype that no longer exists, since prototype.patch
    carries the fix. It is an in-session measurement and cannot be re-run from
    anything here. Restoring it means reverting `mbWallTangents` to a one-sided
    `wall[b] - wall[a]`.
  * "Visibly better round the nose" is a judgement on two rendered PNGs
    (tools/scripts/view_mesh_vtk.py over the window -0.3 1.3 -0.6 0.6). The
    images are not committed. The conforming, non-inverted, quality-line half of
    that sentence IS in (2), (3) and rows.json.
